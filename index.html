About the studio

Sharp vt Creation

Sharp vt Creation is a photography studio in NN Garden, Washermanpet, Chennai, dedicated to turning real moments into photographs that feel timeless, personal and beautifully crafted.

With the experience and creative direction of V. Veerababu, the studio covers weddings, engagements, outdoor portraits, baby milestones, maternity sessions and other family celebrations with a personal, story-first approach.

Alongside photography, the studio also offers frames, passport photos and customized gifts, making it a convenient destination for preserving and presenting special memories.
